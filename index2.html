About MOA

A Legacy of Excellence in Optometric Care

The Meerut Optometrist Association (MOA) was founded in 2008 by a visionary group of optometry professionals who recognized the urgent need for an organized body to elevate the standards of eye care in Meerut and the surrounding districts of Western Uttar Pradesh. What began as a modest gathering of twelve practitioners has grown into a robust association of over 150 active members, each dedicated to the advancement of optometric science and clinical practice.

In its early years, MOA focused on building a network of practitioners and establishing continuing education programs. The founding members — drawn from Meerut's prominent eye hospitals and private clinics — envisioned an association that would not only serve its members but also act as a bridge between the optometry community and the public, promoting awareness about eye health and the critical role of optometrists in primary vision care.

Today, MOA is affiliated with the Uttar Pradesh Optometric Association (UPOA) and the Indian Optometric Association (IOA), and is recognized as one of the most active regional optometry bodies in Northern India. The association regularly organizes clinical workshops, public vision screening camps, and academic conferences that draw participants from across the state.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

Guided by a commitment to clinical excellence, community service, and professional growth, MOA pursues a clear and purposeful mission.

Clinical Excellence

To promote and uphold the highest standards of optometric practice through continuous professional development, evidence-based clinical protocols, and peer-reviewed research, ensuring every patient receives world-class vision care in Meerut.

Community Eye Health

To bridge the gap in eye care accessibility by organizing free vision screening camps in rural and underserved areas of Western Uttar Pradesh, with a focus on early detection of refractive errors, glaucoma, and diabetic retinopathy.

Professional Education

To provide structured continuing education programs including workshops, seminars, and hands-on training sessions that keep our members at the forefront of advances in optometry, contact lens technology, and diagnostic imaging.

Advocacy and Regulation

To advocate for the recognition and regulation of optometry as an independent health profession in Uttar Pradesh, working with government bodies to establish licensing standards and practice boundaries that protect both practitioners and patients.

Member Welfare

To foster a supportive professional community that offers mentorship for young optometrists, career guidance, ethical practice support, and a collaborative environment where experienced practitioners and newcomers learn from each other.

Research and Innovation

To encourage and facilitate clinical research among our members, publishing findings in national and international journals, and embracing emerging technologies such as tele-optometry and artificial intelligence in diagnostic screening.
